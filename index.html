03 · Product

Product strategy: NINJAGO elemental charms

Five elements. One universe.

A Design & features

  • Modular structure. Charms are sold individually and clip onto bracelets, bag chains or keychains (snap-on or carabiner clips).
  • Elemental charms. Fire (Kai), Lightning (Jay), Ice (Zane), Earth (Cole), Energy (Lloyd) and Water (Nya).
  • Rare / secret chase charms. Transparent or glitter-coated “Golden Lloyd” or “Spinjitzu Effect”, with only a 5% chance to pull from boxes.

B Sales model: gamification & collecting

  • Blind bag / blind box. Sold in random boxes to enhance the collecting experience.
  • Hot Topic exclusive. Limited-edition special charms available only in Hot Topic stores and on their website.
